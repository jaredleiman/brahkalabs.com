Stranded

Flight cancelled or delayed? Find out what you're owed — right inside Muse.

Stranded is a Muse connector that helps travelers deal with flight disruptions:

  • Live flight status — real-time status and delay information for your flight.
  • Eligibility checks — see whether you're owed a refund or compensation under US DOT rules or EU261/UK261.
  • Claim drafting — get a ready-to-send claim based on your flight details.
  • Expense tracking — log meals, hotels, and rebooking costs while you're stuck.

Pricing

Status checks and eligibility assessments are free. If you ask Stranded to pursue a claim for you, the fee is 30% of what is actually recovered — and nothing if nothing is recovered.
