Where the hours go

The same pattern shows up in almost every shop we talk to. A bid or an RFQ comes in as a stack of PDFs. Somebody reads plans, inspection reports, or prints by hand, builds the numbers in a spreadsheet, and types the quote up from scratch. It works, because the people doing it are good. It just eats the hours those people could spend on work that actually needs them, and it walks out the door with anyone who retires.

That is the work we build systems for: reading the documents, assembling the first draft of the estimate or quote, and putting the information where the next person can find it. Your people still make the calls and set the prices. They stop doing the typing.
