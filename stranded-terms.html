Stranded — Terms of Service

Operated by Brahka Labs. Effective October 31, 2026.

The service

Stranded is a flight-disruption assistant available through Muse. It can register a trip, check flight status, assess possible eligibility for refunds or compensation (including under US DOT rules and EU261/UK261), draft claim letters, track disruption expenses, and rank rebooking options that you or your assistant provide.

Not legal advice

Stranded is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Eligibility results are informational estimates based on the details provided and the published rules; airlines make the final decision on every refund and claim, and no outcome is guaranteed.

Fees

Flight status checks and eligibility assessments are provided without charge. If you engage Stranded to pursue a claim on your behalf, the fee is contingency-based: 30% of the compensation or refund amount actually recovered, agreed with you before any filing. If nothing is recovered, you owe no fee. Any charge is collected through a Stripe payment link presented to you in advance; you are never charged without seeing the amount first.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of the flight, booking, and expense information you provide, and for your own accounts and bookings with airlines. Stranded does not book, change, or cancel flights.
