Stranded — Privacy Policy

Operated by Brahka Labs. Effective October 31, 2026.

Stranded is a flight-disruption assistant available through Muse. It checks flight status, assesses refund and compensation eligibility, drafts claims, and tracks disruption expenses. This policy explains what information the service handles and why.

Information we handle

When you use Stranded, you may provide: a name for your trip or case, flight numbers, travel dates, origin and destination airports, an email address (if you choose to attach one to a case), expense entries (amount, category, and description), and details needed to draft a claim (such as booking references you choose to share). We do not ask for, and you should not provide, payment card numbers, passwords, or government ID numbers through the connector.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate your case: looking up flight status, checking eligibility under rules such as US DOT refund rules and EU261/UK261, drafting claim documents at your request, tracking your logged expenses, and, if you choose to engage the claim service, processing the agreed fee.

Who else is involved

  • Hosting: the service runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database), where case records are stored.
  • Flight data: flight status is retrieved from AeroDataBox via RapidAPI. Only the flight number and date needed for the lookup are sent.
  • Payments: payments are processed by Stripe through Stripe payment links. Stripe handles your payment details directly; we never receive or store full card numbers.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties other than the providers above as needed to run the service.

Retention and your choices

Case records are kept so the service can track an open disruption case. You may ask for your case data to be deleted at any time by emailing jared@brahkalabs.com, and we will delete it unless we are required to retain a record (for example, a payment record) by law.

Eligibility information

Eligibility assessments are informational estimates based on the flight details provided and the published rules. They are not legal advice and do not guarantee any refund or compensation from an airline.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address. Questions: jared@brahkalabs.com.
